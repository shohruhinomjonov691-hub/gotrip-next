# Product

## Register

product

## Users

GoTrip serves travelers booking curated tours and guides/operators who request approval to publish premium travel experiences. Users arrive from discovery, saved tours, bookings, and profile flows, often needing a fast auth step before returning to their original task.

## Product Purpose

GoTrip helps travelers discover destinations, reserve tours, manage bookings and payments, save travel ideas, and interact with guides and the community. Success means authentication feels trustworthy, quick, and consistent with the premium travel experience.

## Brand Personality

Sophisticated, worldly, serene. The product should feel like a luxury travel concierge: polished, calm, secure, and visually led by destination imagery.

## Anti-references

Avoid real-estate/Nestar language, generic SaaS auth screens, loud gradient-only backgrounds, role pickers that expose backend admin/agent roles, and placeholder mobile experiences.

## Design Principles

- Preserve task flow: authentication should return users to the trip, booking, or profile path that brought them there.
- Show travel aspiration without hiding utility: forms remain clear, labeled, and fast.
- Keep trust visible: security, guide approval, and account defaults should be easy to understand.
- Use motion as state feedback, not decoration.
- Respect backend contracts and staged migration boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic form labels, clear inline errors, 44px minimum touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated surfaces.
